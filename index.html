How to Use the Availability Tracker

1. Isolated Per-Supervisor Schedules

Each Team Leader's schedule is stored independently. Working on one supervisor's view—or adding, editing, or deleting supervisors in Manage TLs—never affects or overwrites any other supervisor's plotted sessions.

2. Fast Shift Plotting & Flexible Hours

Click any cell to plot availability, coaching, or breaks regardless of off-shift patterns. Use Auto-Fill Shift to instantly fill scheduled shift hours with Available status, or Insert Lunch to batch-insert meal breaks across working days.

3. Admin Security & Lock Protection

When the schedule is locked by an Admin, all cells, automations, and dragging are completely disabled in read-only mode to prevent unintended changes.
